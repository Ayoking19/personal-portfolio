The Problem Solver's Origin Story

Natural Marketing & The "Classroom Air Freshener"

My interest in marketing and problem-solving started in primary school, not in a textbook. My classmates had a habit of farting in class, and the smell was unbearable. Instead of just complaining about it, I went home, took my dad's empty aftershave bottle, and mixed soap, powder, body cream, and water to make my own air freshener. I ran user testing to check the spray didn't make the smell worse. It didn't. It worked, the class loved it, and the very next day I sold my first custom bottle for 300 Naira. Honestly, I've been doing the same thing since: spotting a problem and building something to fix it.

Scaling Solutions & Tech Translation

That entrepreneurial instinct only grew. In secondary school, I identified a Market Gap for cold drinking water, so I froze bottles overnight and sold them for a profit. In university, recognizing the late night curfew blocked students from buying cold drinks, I rented a fridge, stocked it, and ran a mini-distribution hub. Today, as a Computer Science student, I use that exact same mindset. I know how to structure MySQL databases and tech architecture. I want to use my CS knowledge as a translation tool to manage engineers, define product rules, and market solutions effectively.

Sports Data, Scouting & Gaming

Outside marketing, my life mostly revolves around sports and gaming. I'm a devoted Arsenal fan, and I'm glued to football. I follow the tactical metrics closely and use Wyscout, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI to evaluate talent against strict criteria. When I'm not analyzing sports, I'm playing chess or testing video games. I have a good eye for Quality Assurance. I'll catch the obvious flaws, but I also notice the small details that make a game fun.

The Culture: Friendly, Youthful & Authentic

I'm trustworthy, friendly, and very expressive. I believe the best work happens somewhere fun, youthful, and low-stress. Football journalism, game bugs, a new product to market: when I'm doing what I love, I don't get stressed. My philosophy is simple. Find the problem and build the solution
